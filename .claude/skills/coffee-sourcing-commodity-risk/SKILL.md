# Coffee sourcing & commodity risk

## Market context (Reported, docs/01 and docs/09)
- Arabica futures: record **~$4.41/lb (Feb 2025)**; **~$2.86 (Sept 29, 2026)** and ~$2.95 (early Oct 2026) after a record Brazil crop.
- Tariffs: coffee is **exempt** from US tariffs on Brazil (executive order effective Nov 13, 2025; the July 22, 2026 Section 301 action also exempts it). Instant coffee's status is conflicting; check the Federal Register.
- Risk: a possible "super" El Niño affecting Brazil's 2027/28 crop. Retail and roasted prices lag futures.

## Sourcing options
1. Local roaster partnership (fastest; check Miami roasters, cupping, freshness, private label).
2. Regional or national specialty roaster (consistency, equipment programs).
3. Roast in-house (higher margin and story, but capex, permits, and possible FDACS involvement for wholesale).

## Contract checklist
Fixed-price period (6-12 months) or a C-market-plus-differential formula, volume minimums, roast-to-delivery freshness, blend consistency, an equipment or training program, exit terms.

## Risk controls
Keep 2+ approved coffees, review prices every quarter, keep a margin buffer in menu prices, and track $/cup for every drink.

## Output
A supplier comparison table ($/lb roasted, minimums, terms, quality score) and the resulting $/cup for the core drinks.
