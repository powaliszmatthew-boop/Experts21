# Miami permitting

Full detail and sources: `docs/06-permits-licenses.md`. **Confirm the jurisdiction first.** Each municipality (City of Miami, Miami Beach, Coral Gables, Doral...) and unincorporated Miami-Dade has its own rules.

## Sequence (City of Miami)
1. Entity and EIN (Sunbiz, IRS).
2. Zoning check before signing the lease. The tenant must confirm restaurant use.
3. DBPR plan review (no charge).
4. Building permits, DERM grease (FOG) review, WASD review, impact fees.
5. Construction and inspections (including fire).
6. Certificate of Use, issued only after construction. An Occupant Load Certificate is needed if occupancy is 50 or more.
7. DBPR food service license and opening inspection.
8. City Business Tax Receipt (the city says this can take several months).
9. Miami-Dade Local Business Tax Receipt (both city and county are required).
10. Florida DOR sales tax registration (DR-1), for each location.
11. Sign permits. 12. Sidewalk café permit (optional). 13. Alcohol license (optional). 14. Annual fire inspection.

## Fees (Verified: DBPR)
Non-seating $242/yr; seating 1-49 $262; 50-149 $273; application $50. The half-year fee applies when you apply 6 months or less before renewal.
County LBTR restaurant tiers (Reported): $45 (1-30 seats), $90 (31-74), $135 (75-149), $180 (150+) inside a municipality.
Sidewalk café: $11.50/sf/yr on a 2014 form; check the current fee.

## Planning rule
Expect 4-9 months from lease signing to opening when construction is involved (Estimate). Keep a tracker: step, agency, submitted date, status, blocker, owner.
