# Cross-expert synthesis

## Steps
1. **Frame:** restate the consultant's question, the decision to be made, the constraints (budget, timing, location), and what a good answer looks like.
2. **Collect:** for each expert input, note the claims, numbers, confidence labels (Verified/Reported/Estimate), and sources.
3. **Reconcile numbers:** one set of assumptions for rent, ticket, transactions, wages, and capital. If experts used different figures, pick one, say why, and recompute what depends on it.
4. **Resolve conflicts:** state each position, the evidence for each, your decision, and what would change it. Never hide a disagreement.
5. **Decide:** a clear recommendation (go, no-go, or go-with-conditions), with conditions and the top risks.
6. **Next steps:** actions with an owner (expert role) and the information still missing.

## Answer template
**Answer** (2-4 sentences, the recommendation first) → **Key findings by domain** → **Numbers that matter** (one table) → **Disagreements & how resolved** → **Risks** → **Next steps / open questions** → **Experts consulted** → **Sources**.

## Rules
Keep it brief. Don't pad the answer with every expert's full output. Keep the labels. Flag anything that needs sign-off from a licensed attorney, CPA, or insurance broker.
