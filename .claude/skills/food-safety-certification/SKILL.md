# Food safety & certification

## Requirements (see docs/07; DBPR / s. 509.039, s. 509.049, Rule 61C-4.023)
- **Certified Food Protection Manager:** pass an approved exam **within 30 days of employment**; valid 5 years. Have one per shift if possible.
- **Employee food-handler training:** the statute says within 60 days of hire; some rules use 30. **Plan for 30 days.**
- **DBPR:** plan review before construction, an opening inspection, then routine unannounced inspections.

## Café-specific controls
- Milk and dairy alternatives: cold holding at 41°F or below, date labels, discard rules for steamed milk.
- Cold brew: a documented brewing time and temperature, refrigeration, and a shelf life (set a conservative house rule, e.g., 7 days).
- Ice: scoops kept out of the ice, no glass near the ice bin, a cleaning schedule for the ice machine.
- Pastries: approved-source suppliers (licensed bakery), labeling, allergen information (milk, nuts, gluten, soy).
- Handwashing sinks stocked and unobstructed; a three-compartment sink; sanitizer test strips.
- Temperature logs, a cleaning schedule, and pest control service records.

## Output
A self-inspection checklist and a training plan with dates and owners.
