# Small-business financing

## Need (Estimate, docs/04)
Total ~$226k-$568k, including a working-capital reserve of at least 6 months of fixed costs; permitting can delay revenue by months.

## Options
- **SBA 7(a):** a common route for small business startups; lenders expect an owner equity injection, a business plan, projections, and personal guarantees. Check current SBA rules and rates before advising.
- **Equipment leasing or financing:** for the espresso machine, grinders, refrigeration. Preserves cash at a higher total cost.
- **Roaster equipment programs:** equipment in exchange for a bean contract. Check the exit terms.
- **Landlord TI allowance and free rent:** effectively financing; negotiate hard.
- **Friends, family, or angel equity:** set the terms carefully (with the corporate attorney); consider convertible notes or revenue-based financing.

## Lender-ready package
Executive summary, market evidence (docs/01-03), site and lease terms, startup budget, a 3-year P&L and cash flow (monthly for year 1), break-even and sensitivities, management bios, personal financial statements.

## Rules
Never size the reserve below the pre-opening rent plus 6 months of fixed costs. Show the debt service in the P&L, because thin operating margins (~8% base case) can't carry heavy debt.
