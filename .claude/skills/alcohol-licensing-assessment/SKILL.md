# Alcohol licensing assessment

This was not researched in depth in docs/. Verify everything with DBPR's Division of Alcoholic Beverages & Tobacco and the local zoning office.

## What to assess
1. **License type and cost:** the Florida beer-and-wine license for on-premises consumption, fees, and processing time (verify current DBPR rules).
2. **Local rules:** zoning, distance from schools and churches, hours (City of Miami and other municipalities differ).
3. **Tax effect:** an on-premises liquor license triggers the **Miami-Dade 1% food & beverage tax** (outside Miami Beach, Surfside, and Bal Harbour, which have resort taxes). See florida-tax-compliance.
4. **Insurance:** liquor liability coverage and higher GL premiums.
5. **Operations:** staff age and training (responsible vendor programs), ID checks, storage.
6. **Economics:** incremental evening revenue vs. license, tax, insurance, and labor costs.

## Output
A go/no-go table with costs, timelines, and the revenue needed to justify it.
