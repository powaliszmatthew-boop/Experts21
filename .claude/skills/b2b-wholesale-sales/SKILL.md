# B2B & wholesale sales

## Target segments
Brickell and Downtown offices, hotels (28M visitors a year), restaurants and bakeries without a coffee program, coworking spaces, universities, event planners.

## Offers
Catering (batch-brew boxes, Cuban colada trays, pastries), office coffee subscriptions, retail bags (private label from the roaster), wholesale beans (if roasting in-house).

## Regulatory check
Wholesaling or manufacturing food can bring in **FDACS** in addition to DBPR (docs/06). Confirm with the permitting expediter before selling wholesale.

## Sales process
1. Build a list of 50 target accounts near the shop.
2. Offer free tastings and samples; propose a pilot.
3. Price sheet with volume tiers; payment terms; delivery radius.
4. Contracts reviewed by the corporate attorney.
5. Track the pipeline: lead → tasting → pilot → recurring account; forecast monthly B2B revenue for the financial analyst.
