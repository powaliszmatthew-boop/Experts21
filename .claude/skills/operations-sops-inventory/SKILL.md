# Operations SOPs, scheduling & inventory

## Labor model (Estimate, docs/05 and docs/07)
About 27 labor hours/day (2 staff off-peak, 3 at peak), about 810 hours/month, roughly 32% of $45k sales. Schedule from transactions by hour; review every week.

## Core SOPs
- **Opening:** equipment on, espresso dial-in, temperature log, par check, cash count, mobile ordering switched on.
- **Peak:** fixed positions (register, bar, cold bar or floater), a mobile-order handling rule.
- **Closing:** clean and backflush, milk discard, ice machine and drains, cash reconciliation, alarm.
- **Weekly/monthly:** deep cleaning, grinder burr check, a water filter change on schedule, pest service.
- **Hurricane (June 1-Nov 30):** shutters, secure outdoor furniture, save or discard perishables, back up the POS, staff contact tree, a reopening checklist.

## Inventory
Par levels for coffee, milk and alt-milks, cups and lids by size, syrups, and food. Count critical items daily and everything weekly. Track waste (milk, pastries) and keep COGS within the target.

## Output
Checklists ready to print in English and Spanish, a weekly schedule template, and a par sheet.
