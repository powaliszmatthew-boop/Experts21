# Social & local search marketing

## Channels (docs/08)
- **Instagram/TikTok:** weekly short videos (latte art, seasonal drinks, staff, the neighborhood), in English and Spanish. Iced Cuban drinks have proven viral appeal.
- **Google Business Profile:** complete categories, hours, menu, photos, posts; reply to every review within 48 hours.
- **Owned app and SMS/email** from the waitlist; loyalty pushes.
- **Delivery apps:** separate menu pricing to cover commissions.

## Launch timeline
- 8 weeks out: accounts live, coming-soon signage (permit), waitlist sign-ups.
- Soft opening: invite local creators; collect early reviews.
- Grand opening: launch drink, free cafecito hour, a live event.
- First 90 days: weekly content and a process to ask for reviews.

## KPIs
Followers to visits (promo codes), share of orders by app, Google rating and review count, repeat rate. Monitor competitors' social media and reviews for themes.

## Budget
Launch $5k-$15k; ongoing about 2% of sales (Estimate).
