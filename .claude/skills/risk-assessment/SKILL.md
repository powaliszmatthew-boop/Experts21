# Risk assessment

## Method
Score each risk for likelihood (1-5) and impact (1-5); priority = L × I. Assign an owner, a mitigation, an early-warning trigger, and a review date.

## Top risks from research (docs/09-risks.md)
1. High rent and losing the lease (county $50.27/sf, Brickell $84.96/sf; 3.1% vacancy; Panther and Suite Habana lost leases in 2026).
2. Running out of cash before the ramp-up (months of permitting; startup cost ~$226k-$568k).
3. Labor cost inflation ($15 minimum wage, CPI-indexed from 2027).
4. Competition and price anchoring (ventanitas, expanding chains, drive-thrus).
5. Permitting delays and cost overruns (CU after construction; DERM FOG).
6. Hurricanes, flooding, insurance.
7. Coffee price volatility.
8. Seasonality and tourist mix.
9. Regulatory and tax surprises (the alcohol trigger, municipal differences).
10. Staffing and turnover.

## Output
An updated register table (risk, L, I, score, owner, mitigation, trigger), plus the 3 risks that most affect the current decision.
