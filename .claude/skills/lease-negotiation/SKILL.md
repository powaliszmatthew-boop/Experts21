# Lease review & negotiation

Research-level guidance only; a licensed Florida attorney must review before signing.

## Must-haves
- **Contingencies:** zoning for café use, permits, the Certificate of Use, and DERM grease approval. The tenant can terminate or delay rent if these are not obtained.
- **Rent start:** free rent through the permitting and build-out period (City of Miami licensing can take months), or rent starts at opening.
- **TI allowance** sized to the build-out; landlord's work letter for MEP and grease trap.
- **Escalators** capped (e.g., ≤3%/yr); CAM caps and audit rights.
- **Term:** a long initial term with renewal options at set or capped rent.
- **Personal guarantee:** limited (burn-off after 24-36 months, or capped at X months' rent).
- **Use and exclusivity:** a broad food and beverage use; an exclusive on coffee in the center if possible.
- **Assignment/sublet** allowed when the business is sold.
- **Casualty and hurricane:** rent abatement while the premises can't be used; termination if not restored within N days.
- **Taxes:** Florida repealed sales tax on commercial rent for periods from Oct 1, 2025 (HB 7031); make sure none is billed.

## Watch-outs from the market
Vacancy is low (3.1%), so landlords have leverage. Panther Coffee (Sunset Harbour) and Suite Habana (Wynwood) both lost their leases in 2026, so renewal rights matter.

## Output
A redline issue list ranked by money at stake, with a recommended ask and a fallback position for each term.
