# Brand strategy & bilingual content

## Recommended positioning (to test; docs/08)
**"Specialty coffee, Miami style."** Specialty-grade beans in Miami's own formats (cafecito, cortadito, colada, iced Cuban drinks) next to modern specialty drinks. Bilingual, fast, and neighborhood-rooted.

## Why
70.6% of residents are Hispanic or Latino, and Cuban coffee culture is the local default. National trends favor iced drinks, app ordering, and convenience.

## Brand toolkit
1. Positioning statement, audience, proof points, personality.
2. Name and trademark check (with the corporate attorney); domain and social handles.
3. Visual identity: logo, colors, type, photo style; signage (needs permits) and menu boards.
4. Voice: natural bilingual copy, Spanglish where it feels authentic. Never machine-translate; have native speakers review.
5. Packaging: cups, retail bags, merch.

## Copy rules
Menu items in English and Spanish, short and readable. Keep culturally specific names (cafecito, colada). Check the copy against how people order at ventanitas.
