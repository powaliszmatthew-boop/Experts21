# PR, partnerships & events

## Press targets
Miami New Times, Time Out Miami, Miami & Beaches (GMCVB) guides, neighborhood newsletters, Axios Miami, WLRN. Pitch angles: specialty cafecito, the founder's story, a signature drink, community impact.

## Partnerships
- Universities: UM (Coral Gables), FIU, MDC: student discounts, study nights.
- Run and cycling clubs: a weekend meet-up point.
- Wynwood art walks (second Saturdays) and local artists: rotating art on the walls.
- Local bakeries: a pastry program with co-branding.
- Coworking spaces and offices: these also become B2B leads.

## Events
Soft opening (friends, neighbors, creators), grand opening (free cafecito hour, DJ, artist), monthly cuppings or latte-art throwdowns.

## Output
A 90-day PR and community calendar with owners, a media list, and pitch drafts.
