# Menu engineering & pricing

## Cost per cup
Cost per drink = coffee dose (g) × $/g + milk or alt-milk + syrup + cup, lid, sleeve, straw + ice and water allowance.
- Coffee $/g = roasted $/lb ÷ 453.6. Get the roasted price from the coffee sourcing specialist.
- Price from your own cost, not from commodity headlines: retail prices lag futures.

## Targets (Reported benchmarks, docs/05)
- Beverage COGS 20-30%; blended COGS (with food) 25-35%.
- Average ticket $6-$12, depending on area and food attach rate.

## Menu architecture
1. **Entry/value:** cafecito, cortadito, colada (shared shots) made with specialty beans. These compete with ventanita prices.
2. **Core espresso:** latte, cappuccino, flat white, café con leche. Oat milk is popular; price the alt-milk upcharge.
3. **Iced and cold first:** cold brew, iced lattes, iced Cuban drinks. Miami's climate and Gen Z demand favor these.
4. **Signature and seasonal:** 1-3 rotating drinks for social media.
5. **Slow bar:** pour-over or single origin at a premium.
6. **Food:** pastelitos, pan de bono, empanadas, croissants (local bakery wholesale), to lift the attach rate.

## Menu matrix
Classify items by popularity × margin (Stars, Plowhorses, Puzzles, Dogs). Keep winners, reprice Plowhorses, cut Dogs. Review every quarter and when the minimum wage changes each Sept 30.
