# Entity & contracts

Research-level guidance only; a licensed Florida attorney drafts and reviews.

## Entity setup
1. Choose the entity (LLC is common; compare tax treatment with the accounting advisor).
2. File with the Florida Division of Corporations (Sunbiz); appoint a registered agent; get an EIN.
3. Operating agreement: ownership, capital contributions, voting, transfer limits, buy-sell terms.
4. Business bank account, bookkeeping, and insurance in the entity's name.

## Contract review checklist (each agreement)
- Parties, term, renewal, termination for convenience or for cause.
- Price, adjustments (index or cap), minimums, payment terms.
- Service levels (delivery, freshness, equipment service response time).
- Warranties, liability caps, indemnities, insurance requirements.
- Assignment and change of control (important when selling the business later).
- Dispute resolution and governing law (Florida).

## Key contracts
Roaster or green-coffee supply (fixed-price window, minimums), equipment lease or finance, general contractor, POS/SaaS, wholesale customer terms, investor documents (with the funding advisor).
