# Florida employment law

Research-level guidance only; confirm with an employment attorney or payroll provider.

## Rules (see docs/07-staffing.md)
- **Minimum wage:** **$15.00/hr** since Sept 30, 2026. **Tipped cash wage $11.98** (maximum tip credit $3.02). **Indexed to inflation** each Sept 30 from 2027.
- **Tip credit:** only for employees who customarily receive tips. Requires notice to employees, and tips must make up the gap to minimum wage. Tip pools can't include managers or owners. Many cafés skip the credit and pay full minimum wage plus tips.
- **Overtime:** FLSA, 1.5× over 40 hours/week.
- **Workers' comp:** required for non-construction employers with **4+ employees** (full- or part-time; non-exempt owners count).
- **E-Verify:** required for private employers with **25+ employees** (SB 1718, effective July 1, 2023).
- **I-9** for every hire; federal and Florida posters (including the minimum wage poster); reemployment tax registration.
- **Minors:** Florida child labor hour limits apply to workers under 18.

## Checklist when hiring
Offer letter with the wage and tip policy, I-9 within 3 business days, payroll setup, workers' comp in force, handbook acknowledgment, food-handler training scheduled.
