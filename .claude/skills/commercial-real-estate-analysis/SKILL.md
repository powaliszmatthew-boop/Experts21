# Commercial real estate analysis

## Benchmarks (Reported: Cushman & Wakefield, see docs/05-operating-costs.md)
| Area | NNN asking $/sf/yr | Vacancy | Period |
|---|---|---|---|
| Miami-Dade retail avg | 50.27 | 3.1% | Q3 2026 |
| Brickell | 84.96 | - | Q3 2026 |
| Wynwood/Design District | ~69.88 | 7.9% | late 2025 |
| Coral Gables | ~56.61 | 1.3% | late 2025 |
Add ~$2-4/sf for CAM, taxes, and insurance.

## Checks for every listing
1. All-in occupancy cost = (base + CAM + tax + insurance) × sf. Convert to $/month and to % of projected sales; target ≤10-12%.
2. Space type: a second-generation food space (existing grease trap, plumbing, restrooms, hood) costs far less to build out than a vanilla shell.
3. Zoning allows restaurant or café use (the tenant must confirm this, not the landlord).
4. Flood zone (FEMA), building age, roof and windstorm protection.
5. Visibility, frontage, walk-up window potential, parking and loading, outdoor seating potential.
6. Co-tenancy and nearby traffic generators.

## Output
A one-page site economics sheet: all-in rent, rent as % of base-case sales, break-even transactions/day (from financial modeling), red flags.
