# Specialty & Cuban coffee craft

## Cuban classics, made with specialty beans
- **Cafecito (café cubano):** a short, strong espresso sweetened while brewing; traditionally the first drops are whipped with sugar into an espumita. Decide a house sugar standard and offer an unsweetened option.
- **Cortadito:** cafecito cut with steamed or evaporated milk.
- **Colada:** a larger cafecito served with small cups for sharing. A social ritual (the 3:05 cafecito).
- **Café con leche:** espresso with hot milk; a breakfast staple.
- **Iced versions:** iced cortadito and others have gone viral with Gen Z (Sergio's example).

## Specialty program
- Espresso dial-in: dose, yield, time per recipe; check at every open and at each bag change; record in a log.
- Milk texture standards; latte art for social media.
- Batch brew and pour-over recipes (ratio, grind, water temperature).
- Cold brew: ratio, steep time, filtration, shelf life (with food safety).
- Water: filtration and scale control are essential in South Florida.

## Quality control
Daily tasting, a weekly cupping, a recipe card for every drink in English and Spanish, and a barista sign-off before working peak shifts.
