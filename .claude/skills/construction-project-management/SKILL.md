# Construction project management

## Budget (Estimate, docs/04)
Build-out $60k-$200k (second-generation space to vanilla shell); drawings $10k-$30k. Hold a **10-15% contingency**.

## Process
1. Scope from the test-fit and equipment schedule; get the landlord's work letter.
2. Get 3 bids from contractors with City of Miami/Miami-Dade restaurant experience; compare the line items.
3. Schedule: drawings → DBPR plan review and building/DERM permits → construction → inspections → Certificate of Use → DBPR license inspection → opening.
4. Track the critical path weekly: permit status, long-lead equipment, inspections.
5. Change orders: written, priced, and approved before the work; log their effect on the budget.

## Risk controls
- A second-generation space lowers cost and time.
- Order long-lead equipment early (espresso machine, refrigeration, ice machine).
- Avoid peak hurricane months (Aug-Oct) for critical exterior work, or plan for weather delays.
- Get free rent through construction (see lease negotiation).

## Output
A budget vs. actual table, a schedule with milestones, and a risk log.
