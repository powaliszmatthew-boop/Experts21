# Competitive scanning

## Scan procedure
1. For each candidate site, list every coffee seller within 0.5 and 1 mile: specialty cafés, Cuban cafeterias and ventanitas, bakery-cafés, chains (Starbucks, Dunkin', drive-thrus), and convenience-store coffee.
2. For each record: name, address, format (seats, walk-up window, drive-thru), hours, price of a latte, cortadito, cafecito and cold brew, app ordering, Google rating and review count, and recurring review themes.
3. Classify: direct (specialty), substitute (Cuban cafeteria, bakery), convenience (chain, drive-thru).
4. Summarize density, price bands, and gaps (daypart, format, quality, language, seating).
