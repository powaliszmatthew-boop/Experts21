# POS, ordering, loyalty & delivery

## Why it matters (Reported, NCA)
40% of past-week out-of-home coffee buyers ordered by app (2026, a record); 55% used a drive-thru. Plan for digital orders from day one.

## Selection criteria
1. POS: café features (modifiers, quick keys), offline mode, kitchen and bar tickets, bilingual receipts, hardware cost.
2. Processing: effective rate (the model assumes ~2.75%), any surcharges.
3. First-party online and app ordering with loyalty (avoid marketplace commissions on regular customers).
4. Delivery marketplaces: use menu markups; check the effect on margin.
5. Integrations: accounting, payroll, inventory, scheduling, email/SMS.
6. Reporting: sales by hour, item mix, app share, repeat customers.

## Also
Guest Wi-Fi with a separate network for the POS, security cameras, music licensing, phone and text ordering.

## Output
A comparison table of 3 POS options with monthly cost at the base-case volume, a recommendation, and a setup checklist.
