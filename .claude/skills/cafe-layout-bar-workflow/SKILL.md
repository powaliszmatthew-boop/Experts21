# Café layout & bar workflow

## Principles
1. Linear flow: order → pay → make → hand-off → exit. The queue must not cross the hand-off area.
2. A dedicated **mobile-order pickup shelf** near the door (40% of out-of-home buyers order by app).
3. A **walk-up window (ventanita)** for cafecitos and to-go orders if the site allows it. Check signage and sidewalk rules.
4. Espresso station: grinder, machine, milk fridge, and knock box within one step; one station per 60-80 drinks/hour at peak (Estimate).
5. Cold bar: an ice bin, cold brew taps or kegs, and syrups grouped for iced drinks.
6. Ice machine capacity sized for Miami summers (plan high), with a backup ice supply.
7. Handwashing sink reachable from the bar; the three-compartment sink in the back.

## Seating
Seat count drives the DBPR license tier, plumbing fixture counts, and occupant load (50+ needs an Occupant Load Certificate). Mix bar seats, 2-tops, and a laptop zone with power outlets.

## Deliverables
A test-fit with flow arrows, an equipment schedule (model, utility needs), a peak throughput estimate (drinks/hour), and a list of utility requirements for the architect.
