# Insurance & business continuity

Research-level guidance only; get quotes from a licensed Florida broker.

## Coverage program
- General liability (including product liability); property (contents, improvements) **including windstorm**; **flood** (check the FEMA flood zone of every candidate site; standard property policies exclude flood); business interruption (include civil authority and utility-service interruption); **workers' comp** (required at 4+ employees); liquor liability if adding alcohol; cyber (POS); employment practices (optional).

## Market (Reported, docs/05)
A broker estimate puts restaurant packages at $3k-$15k/yr (unverified). Florida surplus-lines commercial property cost per policy fell **35% in H1 2026** (wind/hail -28%), but premiums remain among the highest in the US.

## Hurricane season: June 1-Nov 30
1. Before: shutters or impact glass, a generator or backup plan, an inventory and data backup, staff contact tree, insurance documents ready, photos of the premises.
2. Watch/warning: secure the outdoor items, stop deliveries, remove or move perishables, shut down equipment safely.
3. After: a safety check, document the damage, notify the insurer, reopening checklist, staff pay policy.

## Lease link
Rent abatement and termination rights after casualty (with the lease attorney).
