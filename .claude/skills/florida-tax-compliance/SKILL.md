# Florida tax compliance

Research-level guidance only; confirm with a Florida CPA.

## Rules (see docs/05 and docs/06)
- **Sales tax:** 6% state + 1% Miami-Dade surtax = **7%**, collected from customers on taxable sales. Register with Florida DOR (Form DR-1) before selling, **for each location**.
- **County 1% food & beverage tax:** on food and drinks consumed on premises at certain establishments **with a liquor license**. It funds the Homeless Trust (~85%) and domestic violence centers (~15%). It does not apply in Miami Beach, Surfside, or Bal Harbour, which levy resort taxes instead.
- **Miami Beach resort tax:** 2% on food and beverage sales (confirm the current rate and any additional 1%).
- **Commercial rent:** the sales tax on rent is **repealed for rental periods from Oct 1, 2025** (HB 7031). Check that the landlord doesn't bill it.
- **Payroll:** FICA 7.65% employer share, FUTA, Florida reemployment tax; tip reporting.
- **Local business tax receipts** (city + county) renew each year, Oct 1 to Sept 30.

## Workflow
1. Confirm the site's jurisdiction, then list every applicable tax.
2. Set up POS tax settings and the filing calendar (DOR frequency is assigned at registration).
3. Flag tax triggers in concept decisions, e.g., adding beer and wine triggers the 1% F&B tax.
