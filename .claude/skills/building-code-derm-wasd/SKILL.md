# Building, DERM & WASD

## Rules (from docs/06; Miami-Dade sources)
- Adding a food-service use, expanding seats, or changing FOG devices triggers **Building plus DERM grease plan review**.
- Food service facilities need an **approved, installed grease trap and a FOG Grease Discharge Operating (GDO) permit**.
- **Impact fees** apply to commercial permits for additions, alterations, and new construction.
- County commercial plan review is about 8 days, with paid expedited review available.
- In 2026 the county is reviewing its FOG rules; check current requirements before design.
- An occupant load of 50 or more requires an **Occupant Load Certificate** (City of Miami).

## Space readiness checklist
1. Existing grease trap: size, permit status, and condition. A former restaurant is best.
2. Water and sewer capacity for the added use (WASD).
3. Electrical service for the espresso machine, ice machine, ovens, and HVAC.
4. Restrooms and ADA compliance; seat count against plumbing fixture requirements.
5. Fire: extinguishers, exits, a hood only if cooking.

## Output
A readiness rating (green/yellow/red) per item, with cost and time risk, for the architect and the financial analyst.
