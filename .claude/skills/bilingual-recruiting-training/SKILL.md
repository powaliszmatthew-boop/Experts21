# Bilingual recruiting & training

## Staffing plan (Estimate, docs/07)
Owner/GM 1, shift leads 2, baristas 5-7, optional baker. 8-11 people total, so workers' comp is required (4+) and E-Verify is not yet (under 25).

## Recruiting
- Bilingual English/Spanish strongly preferred; 75% of residents speak a language other than English at home.
- Channels: local coffee community, MDC and FIU hospitality programs, job boards, referrals, social media.
- Job posts show the pay range, tips, schedule, and growth path, in both languages.
- A paid working interview at the bar; check attitude and speed, then train the skill.

## Training curriculum (2-4 weeks pre-opening)
1. Coffee basics and the house recipes (espresso, milk, Cuban drinks, cold bar).
2. Service standards and bilingual greetings; handling mobile orders.
3. Food safety (food-handler training within 30 days) and allergen basics.
4. POS, cash, opening and closing SOPs.
5. Sign-off by a shift lead before working peak shifts.

## Pay and retention
Wage ladder above the indexed minimum ($15 now, CPI each Sept 30 from 2027): e.g., $16-$20/hr + tips (Estimate). Fair tip pooling, free drinks and beans, predictable schedules, cross-training, and a path to shift lead.
