# 7. Staffing

## Wages (Verified)
- Florida minimum wage: **$15.00/hr** (since Sept 30, 2026). Tipped cash wage: **$11.98/hr** (maximum tip credit $3.02). From **Sept 30, 2027** it is indexed annually to inflation.
- Reported local benchmark (BLS SOC 35-3023, fast-food and counter workers, which includes baristas, May 2025 via a third party): median about $14.23, mean about $15.27, before tips. The new $15 floor is above the old median, so experienced specialty baristas will likely cost **$16–$20/hr plus tips** (estimate).
- **Tip credit caution:** taking the tip credit requires following FLSA and Florida tip rules (notice, tip pooling limits). Get payroll or legal advice; many cafés pay the full minimum wage plus tips.

## Required certifications (Verified: DBPR / statute; note the conflict)
- **Certified Food Protection Manager:** managers must pass an approved exam **within 30 days of employment** (DBPR, s. 509.039, Rule 61C-4.023). Certificates are valid **5 years**.
- **Employee food handler training:** s. 509.049 says employees must be trained **within 60 days of hire**. Some DOH rules use 30 days. Plan for **30 days** to be safe, and confirm with DBPR.

## Employer obligations (Verified)
- **Workers' compensation:** required for non-construction employers with **4 or more employees** (full- or part-time), counting non-exempt owners (Florida CFO, Division of Workers' Comp).
- **E-Verify:** required for private employers with **25+ employees** (SB 1718, effective July 1, 2023). A single shop may stay under 25 at first, but it is good practice anyway.
- Standard: I-9s, federal and state payroll tax registration (Florida reemployment tax), labor law posters including the Florida minimum wage poster.

## Example staffing plan (Estimate: one shop, about 12 hours/day, 7 days)

| Role | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Owner / General Manager | 1 | Certified food manager; scheduling, ordering, quality |
| Shift leads | 2 | Certified food manager preferred; open and close |
| Baristas (full- and part-time) | 5–7 | **Bilingual (English/Spanish) strongly preferred** |
| Baker / prep (optional) | 0–1 | Or buy wholesale pastries from local bakeries |
| **Total headcount** | 8–11 | Workers' comp required; E-Verify not yet |

About 27 labor hours/day (2 staff off-peak, 3 at peak) is roughly 810 hours/month, which is the figure used in [05-operating-costs.md](05-operating-costs.md).

## Hiring and retention notes (Estimate)
- Recruit through local coffee communities, culinary programs (MDC, FIU hospitality) and Indeed. Train with a structured SCA-style barista program.
- Retention tools: tip pooling, a clear wage ladder above the indexed minimum, free drinks and beans, and predictable schedules.
- Expect yearly wage increases: plan payroll to rise with the CPI-indexed minimum from 2027.
