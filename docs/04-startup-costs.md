# 4. Startup Costs

## Published ranges (Reported: vendor and industry guides, mostly national, not Miami-specific)

| Item | Range | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Total, seated café | $80k–$300k | Toast |
| Total | $100k–$350k | The Business Journal (2025) |
| Total, kiosk to roastery café | $25k to $500k+ | Joe Coffee calculator |
| Build-out (usually the largest line) | $30k–$150k | ZenBusiness |
| Equipment package | $25k–$90k (Joe) / $10k–$30k (FitSmallBusiness) | |
| Espresso machine | ~$2k entry; $10k–$20k for quality semi-auto; $15k+ high-end | 7shifts, HowToStartAnLLC |
| Grinders | $500–$2,500 each | ZenBusiness |
| Launch marketing | ~$5k | Poster |

A **second-generation food space** (with existing plumbing, grease trap and hood) costs much less to build out than a vanilla shell.

## Illustrative Miami budget (Estimate: replace with real quotes)

Assumptions: about 1,200 sf, about 30 seats, second-generation space, espresso bar with light food (no hood), rent of about $64/sf/yr all-in.

| Line item | Low | High | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lease deposit + first month | $13,000 | $20,000 | Rent of about $6.4k/mo; landlords vary |
| Architect / engineering / permit drawings | $10,000 | $30,000 | Required for building, DERM and DBPR plan review |
| Build-out / construction | $60,000 | $200,000 | Second-gen at the low end, vanilla shell at the high end; Miami labor premium |
| Coffee equipment (espresso machine, 2–3 grinders, batch brewer, water filtration, refrigeration, ice machine, blenders) | $40,000 | $90,000 | Water filtration is important; ice demand is high in Miami |
| Furniture, décor, signage | $15,000 | $40,000 | Signs need separate permits |
| POS, app ordering, Wi-Fi, security cameras | $3,000 | $8,000 | |
| Opening inventory (coffee, milk, cups, food) | $5,000 | $10,000 | |
| Pre-opening hiring and training payroll | $10,000 | $20,000 | 2–4 weeks of training at $15+/hr |
| Launch marketing | $5,000 | $15,000 | Soft-opening events, influencers, signage |
| Legal, accounting, entity setup, first insurance premiums | $5,000 | $15,000 | |
| **Working capital reserve (about 6 months of fixed costs)** | $60,000 | $120,000 | Covers rent during permitting and a slow ramp-up |
| **Total** | **≈ $226,000** | **≈ $568,000** | |

### Cost-saving levers
- Take over a former café or restaurant space, and negotiate a **tenant improvement (TI) allowance** and **free rent through the permitting period**.
- Lease or finance the espresso machine. Some roasters offer equipment programs tied to bean contracts.
- Buy used refrigeration and smallwares; buy new for the espresso machine and grinders.
- Start with a smaller footprint (a kiosk or ventanita) and add seating later.

### What changed in Florida in 2025
- **The sales tax on commercial rent was repealed** for rental periods starting **Oct 1, 2025** (HB 7031). Before that, the state rate was 2% plus county surtax. Make sure the landlord isn't still charging it.
