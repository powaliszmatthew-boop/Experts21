# 2. Target Customers

## Behavior data (Reported: NCA, unless noted)

- **Mobile ordering:** a record 40% of past-week out-of-home coffee buyers ordered by app (2026), up from 36% (2025).
- **Drive-thru:** 55% bought at a drive-thru (2026), down from a peak of 59% in 2025.
- **Cold coffee:** 32% had cold brew, frozen/blended or nitro in the past week (2025). In Miami's climate, iced drinks should be the core menu, not a side category.
- **Gen Z:** their first cup is often iced, and about 85% add creamer, against about 70% of all drinkers. This favors flavored and milk-based drinks.
- **Local habit:** cafecito culture is a daily social ritual ("3:05 cafecito" at Calle Ocho), and walk-up ventanitas are common. Iced Cuban drinks (e.g., Sergio's iced cortadito) have gone viral with Gen Z.

## Segments (Estimate: our synthesis)

| Segment | Where | What they want | Price sensitivity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Young professionals / remote workers** | Brickell, Downtown, Edgewater, Wynwood | Speed in the morning, Wi-Fi and seats in the afternoon, app ordering, oat milk, iced drinks | Medium |
| **Latin American residents (Cuban, Venezuelan, Colombian, Argentine, etc.)** | Countywide; Doral, Kendall, Little Havana, Westchester | Strong espresso, cortaditos and coladas, Spanish-speaking staff, pastries (pastelitos, pan de bono, empanadas) | High for daily coffee, lower for treats |
| **Tourists** | Miami Beach, Wynwood, Little Havana, Brickell | A destination or "Instagrammable" stop, local identity, merch and retail beans | Low |
| **Students** | UM (Coral Gables), FIU (Westchester/North Miami), MDC (Downtown/Kendall) | Study space, value, late hours | High |
| **Affluent neighborhood regulars** | Coral Gables, Coconut Grove, Key Biscayne, Pinecrest, Miami Shores | Quality, consistency, pastries, a "third place" | Low |
| **Office and wholesale accounts** | Downtown / Brickell offices, hotels, restaurants | Catering, batch brew, bean supply | B2B |

## Implications

1. **Fit in with Cuban coffee culture.** Offer a high-quality cafecito, cortadito and colada made with specialty beans as an entry-price item, next to pour-overs and lattes.
2. **Offer iced drinks and flavors first**, and use seasonal drinks to build social media buzz.
3. **Hire bilingual staff and use bilingual menus.** 75% of residents speak a language other than English at home.
4. **Put speed first:** app ordering at launch, plus a walk-up window if the site allows it.
5. **Choose the site to match the segment.** The neighborhood decides the customer mix, and that sets ticket size and daypart.
