# Expert Roster: Miami Specialty Coffee Shop

This roster lists the advisory agents for the consultant's engagement and the skills they need. It is built on the research in this folder (see [README.md](README.md)). Each agent's **Docs** column lists the research files it relies on, by number (e.g. `05` = [05-operating-costs.md](05-operating-costs.md)).

**25 agents · 30 skills.** Skills are shared: the skill table lists which agents use each skill, and the agent tables list each agent's skills.

> The legal, tax, and insurance agents give research-level guidance only. A licensed Florida attorney, CPA, or insurance broker must sign off before anything is filed, signed, or bound.

## How the agents work together

1. **A01 Engagement Lead** receives the consultant's question and sends it to the right experts (skill S30).
2. **Site decision first:** A05 (site), A04 (competition), A08 (lease), and A13 (break-even) work together on every candidate location, because rent is the top risk ([09-risks.md](09-risks.md)).
3. **Build and permits:** A06, A07, A10, and A12 work in sequence. The Certificate of Use can only be issued after construction ([06-permits-licenses.md](06-permits-licenses.md)).
4. **Product and pricing:** A17 → A18 → A13 is the costing loop: bean cost sets cost per cup, which sets menu price, which feeds the P&L.
5. **Launch:** A22, A23, A24, and A21 run pre-opening; A19 and A20 handle staffing and training; A25 adds B2B revenue after opening.
6. **Always on:** A16 maintains the risk register; A13 and A19 track KPIs against break-even (S24).
