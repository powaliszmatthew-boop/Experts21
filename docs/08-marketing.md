# 8. Marketing

## Positioning (Estimate: recommendation for the team to test)
**"Specialty coffee, Miami style."** Specialty-grade beans in formats Miami already loves (cafecito, cortadito, colada, iced Cuban drinks), served next to modern specialty drinks (pour-over, oat-milk lattes, cold brew, seasonal iced drinks). Bilingual and fast, with a real neighborhood feel.

Why this positioning: 70.6% of residents are Hispanic or Latino and Cuban coffee culture is the local default. National data shows iced and cold drinks, app ordering and convenience all growing (see [02-target-customers.md](02-target-customers.md)).

## Channels

| Channel | Why | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram / TikTok** | Miami's food scene is driven by social media. Iced Cuban drinks have gone viral with Gen Z (Sergio's example) | Weekly short videos: latte art, a seasonal drink, the staff. Post in both English and Spanish. |
| **Google Business Profile + reviews** | Customers search "coffee near me" | Photos, hours, menu; reply to every review. |
| **Own mobile ordering app / online ordering** | 40% of out-of-home coffee buyers order by app (NCA 2026) | Use the POS's native app to avoid third-party fees; add loyalty. |
| **Delivery apps (Uber Eats, DoorDash)** | Reach office and condo customers | Commissions are high, so mark up menu prices on these apps. |
| **Local press and guides** | Miami New Times, Time Out Miami, Miami & Beaches (GMCVB) "best coffee" lists drive tourist traffic | Pitch an opening story and a signature drink. |
| **Community and partnerships** | Neighborhood loyalty | Run clubs, art walks (Wynwood second Saturdays), coworking, UM/FIU students, local bakeries. |
| **Wholesale / office catering** | Second revenue stream, steady volume | Brickell/Downtown offices and hotels. |

## Launch plan (Estimate)
1. **Pre-opening (8 weeks out):** Instagram account plus a construction "coming soon" sign (permit required); collect emails and phone numbers for the waitlist.
2. **Soft opening (1–2 weeks):** friends, neighbors and local creators; test operations.
3. **Grand opening:** free cafecito hour, a launch drink, a local DJ or artist; invite press.
4. **First 90 days:** loyalty app sign-up push, weekly content, a review-request process, and tracking of daily transactions against break-even (about 175/day; see [05-operating-costs.md](05-operating-costs.md)).

## Budget
- Launch: **$5k–$15k** (estimate; one guide suggests about $5k as a minimum).
- Ongoing: **about 2% of sales** (about $1k/mo in the illustrative P&L).

## KPIs to track
Transactions/day, average ticket, % of orders by app, repeat-customer rate (loyalty), Google rating and review count, sales by daypart.
