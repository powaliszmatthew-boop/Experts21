# 3. Competitors

Check every location and status before using this list. Shops open, close and move often.

## Local specialty leaders (Reported)

| Name | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Panther Coffee** | Roasting since 2010 and widely credited with starting Miami's specialty scene. Wynwood flagship at 2390 NW 2nd Ave. Named to a "100 Best Coffee Shops" world list. **Closed its Sunset Harbour (Miami Beach) shop on Feb 28, 2026** after failing to get a new lease. |
| **Per'La Specialty Roasters** | Local roaster. Its café presence is unclear; one source says it sells online and by appointment, and another lists a Coral Gables café. |
| **Vice City Bean** | Frequently cited for top espresso. Sources disagree on its location (Little Haiti vs. Downtown). |
| **Macondo Coffee** | Colombian-focused. 5 Miami-Dade locations plus 1 in the Tampa Bay area (Miami Herald). Shows that non-Cuban Latin coffee can scale here. |
| **Suite Habana** | Cuban-style specialty. **Lost its Wynwood lease (closed end of April 2026)** and planned to reopen in Miami Beach's Crest Hotel Suites. Reopening not confirmed. |
| Zak the Baker, La Colada Gourmet, MIAM | Bakery-café and Cuban-café competitors in Wynwood and Little Havana. |

## Cuban cafeterias / ventanitas (the default local competitor)

Examples are Versailles (credited with the ventanita concept) and Sergio's. These shops are everywhere, cheap and fast, and deeply familiar. They set the baseline price for espresso in Miami. Cuban bakery-café chains are still expanding; for example, **Sanguich** plans Doral, Miami Lakes, Dadeland and Wynwood locations (no confirmed dates).

## National and regional chains (Reported)

- **Starbucks:** 940 stores in Florida (June 2025) and 87 in the City of Miami (2024), per ScrapeHero. In 2025 Starbucks closed some stores, including a mobile-only Downtown Miami store.
- **New or incoming entrants in 2026:**
  - **Paris Baguette:** first Miami café in Wynwood (June 30, 2026), targeting 40–50 South Florida locations.
  - **Cali Coffee** (drive-thru): first Miami-Dade store in Miami Lakes (Nov 2026), its 22nd location.
  - **7 Brew** (drive-thru): Miami named in its 2026 rollout plans; no sites announced.
  - **27 Club Coffee** (Machine Gun Kelly's brand): 10-year lease for 2,778 sf in Wynwood Jungle (51 NW 23rd St). Opening was targeted for Q2/Q3 2026; status unconfirmed.
  - **Sunshine Coffee:** North Beach flagship.
- Dunkin' and other national chains are also present; we did not quantify them.

## Competitive gaps to exploit (Estimate)

1. **Specialty quality at a Cuban format and price:** specialty-bean cafecito and cortadito served from a fast walk-up window.
2. **Neighborhoods with few independents:** many suburbs (Doral, Kendall, Westchester, Miami Lakes) are dominated by chains and cafeterias. Check this with a site-level scan.
3. **Bilingual "third place" with good seating and Wi-Fi.** Many specialty spots in Wynwood are tourist-heavy and seasonal.
4. **Wholesale and office supply** as a second revenue stream.
