# 6. Permits and Licenses

**First, confirm the jurisdiction.** Miami-Dade County contains many separate municipalities: City of Miami, Miami Beach, Coral Gables, Doral and others, plus unincorporated areas. Each has its own zoning, Certificate of Use and business tax receipt rules. This guide uses the **City of Miami** as the example. Most Miami Beach / North Miami / Miami Gardens details do **not** carry over.

## Sequence (City of Miami example)

| # | Step | Agency | Key facts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Form the entity, get an EIN | FL Division of Corporations (Sunbiz), IRS | Standard. |
| 2 | **Zoning check before signing the lease** | City zoning | The city warns that **you, not the landlord**, must confirm restaurant use is allowed at the location. Make the lease contingent on it. |
| 3 | **DBPR plan review** | FL DBPR, Division of Hotels & Restaurants | **No charge** for plan review. Required before building a new food service space. |
| 4 | Building permits + **DERM grease (FOG) review** + WASD water/sewer review | City building dept. / Miami-Dade DERM / WASD | Adding a food-service use or seats triggers Building and **DERM grease plan review**. You need an **approved, installed grease trap and a FOG Grease Discharge Operating (GDO) permit**. Impact fees may apply. County commercial plan review takes about 8 days (expedited review costs extra). In 2026 the county is reviewing its FOG rules, so check current requirements. |
| 5 | Construction + inspections | City / County | Includes fire review. |
| 6 | **Certificate of Use (CU)** | City of Miami (eStart) | Apply **only after construction is complete**. If assembly occupancy is **50 or more**, you also need an **Occupant Load Certificate**. |
| 7 | **DBPR food service license** + opening inspection | FL DBPR | See the fee table below. |
| 8 | **City Business Tax Receipt (BTR)** | City of Miami (eStart) | The city says the full process "can take **several months**." |
| 9 | **Miami-Dade Local Business Tax Receipt** | Miami-Dade Tax Collector (BTExpress) | Businesses inside a city need **both** a city and a county receipt. Restaurant fees go by seats (reported): **$45 (1–30 seats), $90 (31–74), $135 (75–149), $180 (150+)** in a municipality. The receipt year runs Oct 1–Sept 30. |
| 10 | **Sales tax registration** | FL Dept. of Revenue (Form DR-1) | Register **each location** before making sales. 7% combined rate in Miami-Dade. |
| 11 | Sign permits | City (or county ZIP in unincorporated areas) | Fixed signs need a permit. No signs in the right-of-way. |
| 12 | Sidewalk café permit (optional) | City Resilience & Public Works (iPW portal) | Requires the BTR and CU, a furniture plan and a security deposit. A 2014 form listed **$11.50/sf/yr**, so verify the current fee. |
| 13 | Alcohol license (optional, beer and wine) | FL DBPR, Division of Alcoholic Beverages & Tobacco | Not researched here. Adding it triggers the **county 1% food and beverage tax** on on-premises sales. |
| 14 | Annual fire inspection | Fire marshal | Recurring. |

## DBPR food service license fees (Verified: DBPR fee page)

| License | Full year | Half year* |
|---|---|---|
| Non-seating (take-out only) | $242 | $126 |
| Seating 1–49 | $262 | $136 |
| Seating 50–149 | $273 | $141.50 |
| Application fee (new license or change of ownership) | $50 | — |

\*The half-year fee applies if you apply 6 months or less before the next renewal period.

FDACS (Dept. of Agriculture), not DBPR, licenses grocery stores, bakeries and food manufacturers or wholesalers. A **roaster-café that wholesales beans** may need FDACS involvement as well. Confirm this if we roast.

## Food safety certifications
See [07-staffing.md](07-staffing.md): a certified food protection manager within 30 days, and employee food handler training.

## Timeline expectation (Estimate)
Expect **4–9 months from signing the lease to opening** in the City of Miami when construction is involved. Negotiate free rent for the permitting period.
